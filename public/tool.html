About the app

Built for gamers who want higher FPS and cleaner setups — without hunting through random scripts.

Bari Plux Tool is a desktop optimization suite focused on PUBG Mobile emulators. It brings Active.sav graphics patching, custom views and keymapping, GameLoop prep, ADB file tools, and DNS benchmarks into one Liquid Glass interface — with Bari Plux account sync.

Whether you are tuning 90/120 FPS, restoring TVM keymaps, or cleaning shader caches before a match, the Tool keeps the workflow in one place instead of scattered batch files.

Capabilities

Everything you need around the emulator — from graphics to network.

Graphics & FPS

Patch quality, 90/120 FPS, visual style, and lobby enhancements via Active.sav domains.

Custom View

iPad-style layouts, resolution profiles, and TVM_100 keymapping backup & restore.

Match Prep

GameLoop cleaners, Game Boost, reversible Windows/network tunes, and guest packs.
