About PUBG Mobile

PUBG Mobile is a free-to-play battle royale game where up to 100 players fight to be the last person standing. Players can enter a match solo, duo, or with a team of up to four. Each match starts with players parachuting onto one of the maps. The play area shrinks over time, forcing surviving players into tighter zones and more encounters. The last player or team standing wins the match.
